# RideLedger visual direction

## Direction candidates

- **Night Dispatch — 0.07:** A focused operations desk after dark: ink-blue/green panels, cool mint data highlights, precise thin dividers, and route-map cues. Deliberately high-contrast and compact without looking like a trading terminal.
- **Route Paper — 0.04:** A contemporary driver's paper logbook: warm off-white surfaces, dark navy ink, restrained orange stamps, and editorial serif accents that make daily records feel archival.
- **Open Road — 0.08:** An airy daylight workspace: pale sage, soft white cards, confident green totals, and open spacing inspired by fleet dispatch software.

## Chosen direction: Night Dispatch

### Design movement
Contemporary industrial utility with the calm clarity of well-made navigation equipment. The dashboard should feel like dependable field software, not a gaming UI or financial trading screen.

### Core principles
1. Put the rider's actual take-home result first; label every estimate and input so it can be audited.
2. Keep daily entry direct and low-friction, with no sample business metrics or illustrative transaction history.
3. Use consistent currency, date, and unit notation; never imply more precision than is stored.
4. Make navigation and action states legible at both phone and desktop widths.

### Color philosophy
Use deep blue-green ink (`#101E22`) for the shell, raised charcoal-teal surfaces (`#182A2D`), muted slate text, and warm paper-white as the primary text color. Cool mint (`#69D6B4`) marks positive net results and primary actions; amber (`#F1B567`) flags estimated costs and maintenance reserves. Red is reserved for destructive/error states. Keep chart grid lines and dividers quiet.

### Layout paradigm
Persistent desktop sidebar with a compact brand lockup and two purposeful destinations—Ledger and Cost settings—beside a roomy content surface. On mobile, keep the essential content in one column, stack metric cards, and let forms and records scroll naturally without horizontal overflow.

### Signature elements
A small route-ribbon mark paired with a ledger/checkpoint cue; compact uppercase overlines for data categories; a highlighted net-profit summary; and a small “estimate” badge on fuel and maintenance values. No decorative route map or sample-data graph.

### Interaction philosophy
Every form clearly distinguishes required inputs from derived values. Validate inline, keep the date explicit, confirm destructive deletion, and give concise saved/error notices. Date-range changes update both metrics and charts from the same stored-record query.

### Animation
Use brief, low-amplitude transitions for navigation and card emphasis only. Avoid looping, parallax, and celebratory movement around financial values.

### Typography system
Use a clean system sans stack for interface copy and tabular numerals for monetary and distance figures. Titles use a slightly tighter weight/spacing; field labels remain plain and readable. Do not require a remote font service.

### Brand essence and voice
**RideLedger** is a trustworthy daily account book for riders who need to see what each workday really earned. Voice: practical, respectful, transparent about estimates, and never promotional.

### Wordmark / logo
Display the text name “RideLedger” beside a compact route-ribbon emblem. The app icon is a simple, centered route-and-ledger mark on a full-bleed deep blue-green square; no text inside the icon and no pre-rounded badge.

### Signature brand color
Cool mint `#69D6B4` against deep blue-green ink `#101E22`.
